Bubble Sort: How It Works

Bubble Sort repeatedly steps through the list, compares adjacent elements, and swaps them if they are in the wrong order. With each pass through the array, the largest remaining unsorted value "bubbles up" to its final position at the end of the list.

💡 Core Decision Rule / Invariant:
O(n²) Average
if (arr[j] > arr[j + 1]) swap(arr[j], arr[j + 1]);

Comparing adjacent pairs: If the left element is strictly greater than the right element, their order is inverted, requiring an immediate swap.
